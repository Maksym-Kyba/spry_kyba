Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

This Meetings application is a student educational and demonstration project. It is not intended for commercial use or the processing of sensitive personal information.

Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google or email and password through Amazon Cognito, we receive basic authentication profile information including your email address and authentication identifier. This information is used solely to authenticate you into the demonstration application and associate your created meetings with your session.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively to facilitate app functionality during class and lab demonstrations. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

Data Retention and Security

All authentication credentials are handled by Amazon Cognito and Google Identity Services. Because this is an educational demo environment, data and user accounts may be purged periodically when demo infrastructure is reset.

Contact

If you have any questions or wish for your test account to be removed, please contact the student course administrator.
